## How Instant Replay Works

Instant Replay maintains a bounded rolling buffer of recent gameplay or desktop activity without filling your disk with hours of unused footage.

- **Linux**: `gpu-screen-recorder` runs in replay mode and flushes the buffer to disk when ClipForge sends `SIGUSR1` to the managed child PID.
- **Windows**: A bounded segmented rolling ring (`-f segment -segment_time 2 -segment_wrap <slots>`) writes short segments and concatenates them into a clean MP4 on save.

## Global Hotkeys

- **Linux (Wayland)**: Uses the XDG Desktop Portal `GlobalShortcuts` interface via `zbus`.
- **Linux (X11)**: Falls back to X11 key grabbing when the portal is unavailable.
- **Windows**: Uses Win32 `RegisterHotKey`.
- **In-App Shortcuts**: `F9`, `F10`, and `F11` remain available whenever the ClipForge window is focused.
