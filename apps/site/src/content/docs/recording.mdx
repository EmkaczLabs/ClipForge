## Capture Backends

ClipForge uses platform-native capture pipelines rather than Electron or browser-based screen capture:

- **Linux**: Supervises `gpu-screen-recorder` as an independent child process (`very_high` quality, `-bm auto`).
- **Windows**: Uses Windows Graphics Capture (`WGC`) with D3D11/DXGI frame pooling and hardware encoders (`NVENC`, `AMF`, `QSV`, or `libx264` fallback).

## Multi-Track Audio Routing

ClipForge supports deterministic audio routing across `Auto` and `T1`–`T4` tracks:

- **Auto**: Mixes system audio and microphone into the primary track (`0:a:0`) for universal player compatibility.
- **Separate Tracks (`T2`–`T4`)**: Isolates desktop audio, microphone, or application streams into discrete MP4 audio tracks for post-processing.

During preview in the **Editor**, ClipForge's `AutoMix` player blends all active tracks automatically.
