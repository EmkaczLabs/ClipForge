## The Obsidian Forge Workspace

ClipForge is organized into five primary sections accessible from the left navigation rail:

1. **Library** — Browse, search, filter, and inspect local clips stored in your SQLite catalog.
2. **Editor** — Frame-accurate preview, multi-track `AutoMix` playback, and Fast or Precise trimming.
3. **Recording** — Configure display/window sources, audio tracks, and start active recording or Instant Replay.
4. **Uploads** — Optional queue for uploading clips to a self-hosted `ClipForge-Server` instance.
5. **Settings** — Manage storage directories, global hotkeys, audio routing, and optional cloud server connection.

## Your First Clip

1. Open **Settings** and confirm your Clips Output Folder.
2. Switch to **Recording** and select your monitor or window target.
3. Start **Instant Replay** or **Record**, then press your save hotkey (`F9` by default when focused, or your configured global shortcut).
4. Your saved MP4 appears automatically in **Library** with a generated thumbnail and metadata.
